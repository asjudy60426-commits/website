Cultural Trails in Java

Immerse yourself in Javanese culture with this wholly enriching stay

Wake to Borobudur on the horizon with a three-night stay. Enjoy airport transfers, breakfast and three daily cultural activities of your choice.  
 

Until 31 March 2028

Your three-night stay includes: 

  • Round trip transfers from Yogyakarta International Airport
  • Scheduled cultural lectures led by Amanjiwo’s anthropologist 
  • Daily breakfast
  • Daily traditional afternoon tea
  • In-room refreshments and non-alcoholic beverages  
  • Scheduled shuttle around Borobudur area (advanced booking required) 

Three activities per stay. Choices include: 

  • A tour of Borobudur
  • A temple tour of the Kedu Valley
  • A Javanese blessing ceremony
  • A Javanese / Aksara Jawa script-writing class
  • A Javanese cooking class
  • A Javanese dinner
  • A breakfast at Gubug Sawah, a traditional pavilion fringing the rice field
  • A hike on the Menoreh Hills
  • A morning market tour of Borobudur and Muntilan
  • A village tour on mountain bikes

Upgrade to the Dalem Jiwo Two-Bedroom Villa:

  • Meet & Greet service on arrival at Yogyakarta International Airport
  • Champagne and sweets upon arrival
